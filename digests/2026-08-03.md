# Physics & Maths Digest — 2026-08-03

Monday’s notes pull a late-July PRL dark-matter result into the week’s window, pair it with fresh Mars escape physics and ångström-scale nanoscopy, and add two structural maths preprints — one in Kähler geometry, one in algorithms.

## Top discoveries

### 1. XENONnT sets world-leading S2-only limits on light dark matter
The XENON Collaboration (Physical Review Letters, 30 Jul) analyses 7.83 tonne-years of ionization-only data from XENONnT. Dropping the usual scintillation (S1) requirement and building the detector’s first complete S2-only background model opens nuclear recoils down to 0.5 keVnr and electronic recoils to 0.04 keVee. No excess appears; new 90% confidence limits constrain light WIMPs, axion-like particles, and dark photons, pushing spin-independent sensitivity at 5 GeV/c² toward the solar-neutrino fog.

**Why it matters:** Liquid-xenon experiments are running out of easy parameter space. A trustworthy S2-only background model is the tool that lets them keep searching below the classical WIMP window.

### 2. Kelvin–Helmholtz waves drive bulk atmospheric ion escape at Mars
Zhang and colleagues (Science Advances; covered through 3 Aug) combine MAVEN and Tianwen-1 so one spacecraft watches the undisturbed solar wind while the other records escaping ions. They show Kelvin–Helmholtz boundary waves spawn plasma clouds that carry atmosphere away, mainly on the flank set by the solar-wind electric field.

**Why it matters:** Mars once held a thicker air and liquid water. A concrete wave-driven loss channel helps explain how unmagnetized planets dry out — and what ESCAPADE should target next.

### 3. U-STORM: ångström-precision multicolour imaging with one NIR laser
Mandal, Peng and collaborators (Nature Nanotechnology, 27 Jul; MIT/Broad coverage through 2 Aug) tune small upconverting nanoparticles so they blink spontaneously forever under a single near-infrared laser. Averaging tens of thousands of localizations yields about 0.62 Å precision and simultaneous multicolour maps of membrane protein clusters without special buffers.

**Why it matters:** Conventional dyes bleach and multicolour setups need many lasers. Indefinite NIR blinking on a simple microscope stack opens ångström-scale biology to ordinary labs.

### 4. Kähler MMP and Tosatti’s transcendental base-point-free theorem
Hacon and Xie (arXiv:2607.24986, 27 Jul) run a minimal model program for big gklt Kähler pairs and prove Tosatti’s transcendental base-point-free conjecture as a corollary.

**Why it matters:** The algebraic MMP has reshaped birational geometry for decades; carrying it into the Kähler/transcendental world organises a much wider class of complex manifolds (still a preprint).
