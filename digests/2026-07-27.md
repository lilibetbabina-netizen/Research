# Physics & Maths Digest — 2026-07-27

First direct evidence of a light-induced Floquet topological state in SnTe, plus a classical spacetime crystal caught melting in stages.

## Top discoveries

### 1. Direct evidence of a Floquet topological state in SnTe
Nature Physics (covered 27 Jul 2026): femtosecond pump–probe on tin telluride shows a transient Dirac cone — signature of a light-driven band inversion into a Floquet topological state that vanishes ~100 fs after the pump.

**Why it matters:** Moves Floquet topological insulators from theory/simulation into a concrete, ultrafast experimental platform for light-controlled topology.

### 2. Spacetime crystal melts in three stages
PNAS-covered tabletop experiment (Shanghai Jiao Tong; reported 27 Jul): vibrating-disk spacetime crystal melts with spatial and temporal order collapsing at distinct thresholds via different mechanisms.

**Why it matters:** Shows spacetime symmetries can decouple during melting, a new window on nonequilibrium phase transitions.
