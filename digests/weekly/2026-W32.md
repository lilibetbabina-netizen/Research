# Physics & Maths Weekly — 2026-W32

Across 3–9 August 2026, quantum communication moved from chip-scale metropolitan links to sunlight-powered entanglement and multi-user access networks, while new optical tools reached from ångström imaging to the soft-X-ray water window. Extreme-field and correlated-matter experiments probed vacuum birefringence, Hubbard pseudogaps, and magic-angle graphene, and mathematics delivered new constructions, counterexamples, and resolutions across geometry, topology, and graph theory.

## Themes

### Quantum communication and error-resilient hardware
- Integrated microcombs drove a fully connected, measurement-device-independent quantum-key network over 200 km of fibre ([4 Aug](../2026-08-04.md)).
- A fast controlled-Z gate between dual-rail cavity qubits preserved their strong erasure-noise bias, filling a key hardware gap for error correction ([6 Aug](../2026-08-06.md)).
- Concentrated sunlight produced Bell-quality photon pairs, while a triangular superconducting network demonstrated GHZ states and secret sharing with eavesdrop detection ([7 Aug](../2026-08-07.md)).
- Composable, finite-size continuous-variable QKD ran across a passive multi-user optical access network over 10 km links ([9 Aug](../2026-08-09.md)).

### Ultrafast optics and light–matter control
- U-STORM used indefinitely blinking upconverting nanoparticles and one near-infrared laser to reach about 0.62 Å localization precision in multicolour imaging ([3 Aug](../2026-08-03.md)).
- Photonic altermagnetism appeared without net magnetization, and femtosecond-driven ITO revealed the saturation limits of ultrafast index switching ([4 Aug](../2026-08-04.md)).
- A tuneable tabletop high-harmonic source shifted narrowband EUV and soft-X-ray combs onto selected absorption edges ([7 Aug](../2026-08-07.md)).
- Correlated two-electron recombination extended helium harmonics beyond the single-electron cutoff and into the water window near 280 eV ([8 Aug](../2026-08-08.md)).

### Correlated, topological, and extreme-field matter
- Magnetar X-ray polarization supplied a concrete strong-field-QED test of vacuum birefringence, while a cold-atom Hubbard simulator mapped a pseudogap phase diagram ([6 Aug](../2026-08-06.md)).
- Perfect transmission through a duality defect converted a particle into a twisted-sector string excitation, giving a spin-chain realization of the Callan–Rubakov puzzle ([8 Aug](../2026-08-08.md)).
- Ultrafast photoemission showed the two competing charge-density waves in ErTe₃ recovering through distinct smooth and nucleation-driven mechanisms ([8 Aug](../2026-08-08.md)).
- Atomic-scale Hall-state wavefunctions and entropy plateaus exposed valley polarization and heavy-fermion fingerprints in magic-angle graphene ([9 Aug](../2026-08-09.md)).

### New windows on particles, planets, and precision sensing
- XENONnT's first complete S2-only background model tightened light-dark-matter limits down to sub-keV recoil energies ([3 Aug](../2026-08-03.md)).
- Coordinated MAVEN and Tianwen-1 observations identified Kelvin–Helmholtz plasma clouds as a channel for atmospheric ion escape from Mars ([3 Aug](../2026-08-03.md)).
- Double Chooz made the first measurement of residual antineutrinos from spent nuclear fuel, supporting reactor-shutdown and inventory monitoring ([5 Aug](../2026-08-05.md)).
- An optically levitated nanoparticle registered individual collisions with Kr, Xe, and SF₆ molecules, pointing toward a primary pressure standard and rare-impulse sensing ([9 Aug](../2026-08-09.md)).

### Geometry, topology, and extremal combinatorics
- A Kähler minimal model program proved Tosatti's transcendental base-point-free conjecture, while ReAPR rapidly simplified every published hard-unknot example tested ([3 Aug](../2026-08-03.md); [4 Aug](../2026-08-04.md)).
- Finite-field constructions asymptotically attained the Moore bound for every fixed graph diameter, settling Bollobás's conjecture ([5 Aug](../2026-08-05.md)).
- An explicit period-2, index-8 Brauer class disproved the period–index conjecture in dimension three ([6 Aug](../2026-08-06.md)).
- Uniform Turán densities were shown to contain an interval near one, and Andersen's rainbow-path conjecture was proved for sufficiently large complete graphs ([7 Aug](../2026-08-07.md); [9 Aug](../2026-08-09.md)).

## Standouts

1. **Residual neutrinos from spent fuel** turn a faint post-shutdown signal into a potential safeguards tool ([5 Aug](../2026-08-05.md)).
2. **Sunlight-powered Bell entanglement** suggests lower-energy and space-deployable quantum photon sources ([7 Aug](../2026-08-07.md)).
3. **Ångström-precision multicolour imaging with one NIR laser** could make exceptionally fine biological maps accessible to more laboratories ([3 Aug](../2026-08-03.md)).
4. **Single-molecule collision sensing** advances both primary pressure metrology and searches for rare weak impulses ([9 Aug](../2026-08-09.md)).
5. **Wave-driven atmospheric escape at Mars** sharpens the story of how unmagnetized planets lose air and water ([3 Aug](../2026-08-03.md)).

## Watchlist

- **Polynomial-time Dihedral Coset Problem algorithm** — a preliminary, unrefereed draft with potentially major implications for approximate SVP, LWE, and post-quantum cryptography if verified ([7 Aug](../2026-08-07.md)).
- **Graph k-colouring below the 2ⁿ barrier** — a strong randomized-complexity claim still awaiting community checks ([3 Aug](../2026-08-03.md)).
- **Shortest Vector Problem in 2^0.6039n time** — crypto-relevant classical and quantum lattice algorithms that remain at preprint stage ([5 Aug](../2026-08-05.md)).
- **Period–index counterexample** — an explicit conjecture-breaking construction with broad arithmetic-geometric consequences, currently a preprint ([6 Aug](../2026-08-06.md)).
