# Physics & Maths Digest — 2026-08-10

Monday’s notes pull together a 7–10 Aug cluster that Sunday’s graphene-heavy digest left aside: the first RIXS glimpse of plasmons in a low-valence nickelate, experimental breathing ferroelectricity in Nb₃Cl₈, an NMR demonstration that coarse measurements can reveal quantum causal structure, a theoretical composite Bogoliubov Fermi liquid for half-filled Chern bands, and a 7 Aug arithmetic-geometry preprint proving modularity of higher theta series.

## Top discoveries

### 1. Correlated plasmons observed in a low-valence nickelate
Shen, He, Sears, Božović and collaborators (Physical Review X, 7 Aug) use resonant inelastic x-ray scattering to detect plasmons in metallic trilayer Pr₄Ni₃O₈. Compared with a cuprate at similar doping, the nickelate modes are slower and more heavily damped — consistent with reduced hopping and stronger Coulomb screening — and they soften as temperature rises, whereas cuprate plasmons mainly broaden at fixed energy.

**Why it matters:** Plasmon spectra are a direct readout of charge dynamics. Seeing them in a nickelate parent compound gives a quantitative test of how closely these materials really track the cuprate playbook.

### 2. Breathing ferroelectricity, flat bands and chiral phonons in Nb₃Cl₈
Yu, Sui, Yue and co-authors (Nature Communications, 8 Aug) show that van der Waals breathing-kagome Nb₃Cl₈ hosts breathing ferroelectricity from alternating triangular distortions, thickness-dependent flat-band optics (exciton-like peaks in thin flakes; semiconductor-like edges in thick crystals), and multiple chiral phonons. Temperature trends in phonon and flat-band features signal electronic or magnetic transitions.

**Why it matters:** Switchable polarization, flat bands and chiral lattice modes in one layered crystal offer a compact materials platform for multifunctional nanoscale quantum devices.

### 3. Quantum causal inference from measurements alone
Liu, Vedral, Dahlsten, Lu and colleagues (npj Quantum Information, 10 Aug) discriminate causal structures for a bipartite system at two times using only coarse-grained projective measurements — NMR scattering circuits on four ¹³C spins in crotonic acid — without reset-style interventions. Pseudo-density-matrix analysis identifies structures for partial-swap and fully decohering channels.

**Why it matters:** Classical causal discovery typically needs interventions. Observation-only quantum inference is a practical step for diagnosing influence in quantum networks where resetting is costly or impossible.

### 4. Composite Bogoliubov Fermi liquid in a half-filled Chern band
Shi & Nosov (Physical Review Letters, 7 Aug) propose that in inversion-asymmetric, C₃-symmetric Chern bands — where anomalous composite Fermi liquids have been reported — composite fermions can form a paired state that still supports neutral Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces. The phase would be incompressible with quantized Hall conductance, no quantum oscillations versus field or doping, and topological torus degeneracy despite gapless neutral quasiparticles.

**Why it matters:** The construction sits between a gapless composite Fermi liquid and a fully gapped Pfaffian, giving experiments a sharper checklist for pairing in zero-field Chern bands.

### 5. Modularity conjecture for higher theta series proved
Feng, Yun & Zhang (arXiv:2608.07173, 7 Aug) prove the Modularity Conjecture for higher theta series on moduli stacks of Hermitian shtukas, and a stronger supermodularity statement for general linear shtukas, via a Trace Conjecture for low-corank Hitchin stacks.

**Why it matters:** The result ties special cycles on shtuka moduli to automorphic forms, closing a central existence question in that arithmetic program (preprint).
