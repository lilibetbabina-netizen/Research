### 1. Floquet topological SnTe (week standout)
First direct evidence of a light-induced Floquet topological state via band inversion in SnTe (Nature Physics).

**Why it matters:** On-demand ultrafast topology for quantum materials research.

### 2. Room-temperature phonon focusing
Boron arsenide carries heat in ray-like phonon paths at ambient conditions (Nature Physics).

**Why it matters:** Foundation for quantum thermal engineering in hot chips.

### 3. Electron lighthouse photocurrents
Two-color light steers directional electron beams in a semiconductor without applied voltage (PRL).

**Why it matters:** Optical-only current control for future optoelectronics.
