Bright isolated soft-X-ray pulses compressed to 18 attoseconds

What changed
Gao, Hasan, Liang and collaborators generated isolated 18 ± 2 attosecond pulses from a post-compressed ytterbium laser. The 50–320 eV spectrum reaches the carbon K-edge at a generation-stage flux above 10¹² photons per second; angle-resolved helium streaking validates the pulse retrieval.
Why it matters
Bright tabletop pulses shorter than one atomic unit of time can resolve electron and inner-shell dynamics on their natural timescales.
Key source
Nature Communications

physicshigh

Pressure distinguishes superconducting phases in UTe₂

What changed
Zou, Shi, Qiu and collaborators use point-contact spectroscopy to track UTe₂ under pressure. Andreev bound states strongly suggest finite odd-kz pairing components, while modelling distinguishes phases through the relative weights of pz- and px(y)-wave pairing.
Why it matters
A symmetry-sensitive signature that evolves with pressure narrows the possible order parameters in a leading spin-triplet and topological-superconductivity candidate.
Key source
Nature Communications
03
physicshigh

Layered Co₁/₄TaSe₂ shows altermagnetic spin splitting

What changed
Sprague, Mondal, Sakhya and collaborators identify Co₁/₄TaSe₂ as a layered altermagnet. Photoemission resolves spin-split Fermi-surface bands, while magnetic measurements find type-A antiferromagnetic order up to 178 K.
Why it matters
The material supplies a layered platform combining zero net magnetisation with spin-split bands for studies of altermagnetic transport.
Key source
Nature Communications

mathsmedium

Banach’s isometric conjecture completed over complex spaces

What changed
Acuaviva and Kania prove that a complex normed space whose fixed-dimensional complex subspaces are mutually isometric must have a Hermitian norm, closing the remaining complex cases and proving a quaternionic counterpart.
Why it matters
The preprint closes a long-running rigidity problem about when lower-dimensional geometry determines the whole space.
Key source
arXiv:2608.18257
05
mathsmedium

Tarski’s additive problem for linear orders solved

What changed
Ervin and Paul prove that nA + mB ≅ kB + lA forces A + B ≅ B + A for arbitrary linear orders and positive integer coefficients, answering Tarski’s 1956 problem.
Why it matters
The preprint closes a 70-year-old question about when a mixed identity forces commutation in the normally noncommutative arithmetic of order types.
Key source
arXiv:2608.20309
06
mathsmedium

The exact asymptotic constant in Schäffer’s matrix inequality

What changed
Houache, Szehr and Zarouf prove that the optimal universal constant Sₙ satisfies Sₙ/√n → √e and explicitly construct Banach-space norms and matrices approaching this 1970 upper bound.
Why it matters
The preprint replaces a five-decade order estimate with the exact first-order asymptotic for inverse-matrix control outside Hilbert space.
Key source
arXiv:2608.20217
