## Findings

### 1. XENONnT sets world-leading S2-only limits on light dark matter
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** The XENON Collaboration (Physical Review Letters 137, 051003; published 30 Jul 2026; DOI 10.1103/2lrq-f6bk) reports a blinded ionization-only (S2-only) dark-matter search with 7.83 tonne-years over 579 days across three XENONnT science runs. With the first complete S2-only background model for the detector, they reach nuclear-recoil sensitivity in [0.5, 5.0] keVnr and electronic recoils in [0.04, 0.7] keVee. No significant excess is seen; 90% CL limits improve constraints on spin-independent/spin-dependent nucleon scattering (3–8 GeV/c²), DM–electron scattering, axion-like particles, and dark photons (e.g. SI cross section above 6.0×10⁻⁴⁵ cm² excluded at 5 GeV/c²).
- **Why it matters:** Closing the light-WIMP window toward the coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering (CE\nu NS) fog, with a full S2-only background model, is a milestone for next-generation liquid-xenon searches.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.11296

### 2. Kelvin–Helmholtz waves drive bulk atmospheric ion escape at Mars
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Zhang et al. (Science Advances; DOI 10.1126/sciadv.aed9072; press coverage through 3 Aug 2026) use simultaneous MAVEN and Tianwen-1 observations to link upstream solar-wind conditions to escaping planetary ions. They identify Kelvin–Helmholtz boundary waves as a driver of bulk atmospheric ion escape, with plasma clouds forming preferentially on one flank set by the solar-wind electric-field direction.
- **Why it matters:** Mars lost most of its ancient atmosphere; pinning a concrete wave-driven escape channel clarifies how solar wind erodes unmagnetized planets and frames follow-on work with ESCAPADE.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/08/260801042816.htm

### 3. U-STORM: ångström-precision multicolour imaging with blinking upconverting nanoparticles
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Mandal, Toll, Ma, Peng et al. (Nature Nanotechnology, VoR 27 Jul 2026; MIT/Broad coverage through 2 Aug) engineer ~10 nm upconverting nanoparticles that blink spontaneously and indefinitely under a single near-infrared laser, without buffers or optical modulation. Repeated localizations yield ~0.62 Å precision (≈88,000 events from one particle) and enable simultaneous multicolour U-STORM of membrane protein assemblies.
- **Why it matters:** Photobleaching and multi-laser complexity have capped localization microscopy; indefinite NIR blinking with one excitation source pushes precision into the ångström regime on a simpler optical stack.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41565-026-02233-x

### 4. Kähler MMP and Tosatti’s transcendental base-point-free theorem
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** Hacon & Xie (arXiv:2607.24986, 27 Jul) establish the minimal model program for big gklt Kähler pairs and, as a consequence, prove Tosatti’s transcendental base-point-free conjecture.
- **Why it matters:** Extends the algebraic MMP toolkit into the Kähler/transcendental setting — a structural advance for classifying complex manifolds beyond projective varieties (preprint).
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24986

### 5. Breaking the 2ⁿ barrier for graph k-coloring
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** Pratt (arXiv:2607.27159, 29 Jul) shows that for every k there exists ε_k > 0 such that graph k-coloring admits a one-sided-error randomized algorithm in time O((2−ε_k)ⁿ). Prior exponential improvements over the Björklund–Husfeldt–Koivisto 2ⁿ·poly(n) bound were known only for k ≤ 6 (concurrent independent work by Zamir is noted).
- **Why it matters:** Coloring is a canonical #P/#SAT-adjacent combinatorial bottleneck; beating 2ⁿ for all k is a clean complexity-theoretic advance (preprint).
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27159
