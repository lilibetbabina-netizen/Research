## Findings

### 1. All-optical photonic time crystal in a plasmonic metamaterial
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Guo et al. (Nature, published 29 Jul 2026) demonstrate the first all-optical photonic time crystal using a surface-plasmon cavity metamaterial at terahertz frequencies. Near-unity, sub-cycle driving modulates carrier kinetic energy and effective mass (up to ~80% of rest mass); spectroscopy shows a Floquet exceptional-point transition into a PTC regime where emergent gain cuts plasmonic losses by more than 50%.
- **Why it matters:** Temporal photonic-band engineering becomes experimentally accessible, with a path toward plasmonic lasing and time-domain control of light–matter coupling.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10825-9

### 2. The piezochiral effect: strain that writes handedness
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Zeng et al. (Nature, 29 Jul 2026) introduce a linear piezochiral coupling C = Σ T_ij ε_ij and show by group theory that uniaxial strain induces chirality in a broad class of achiral crystals that host opposite-handedness fragments in each unit cell. Experiments on AgGaS₂ measure strain-tuned optical activity; compressive vs tensile strain (or orthogonal strain axes) reverse handedness.
- **Why it matters:** Gives a conjugate-field handle on solid-state chirality—analogous to piezoelectricity—useful for spintronics, photonics, and enantioselective materials design.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10845-5

### 3. Non-Abelian electric field and photonic Zitterbewegung on a frequency chain
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Yang, Wong & Yang (Physical Review Letters; phys.org 29 Jul 2026) synthesize a non-Abelian electric field in a polarization-multiplexed, time-modulated fiber ring resonator. Self-heterodyne detection shows Zitterbewegung of the spectral center of mass without external detuning, and its interference with Abelian Bloch oscillations when detuning is added.
- **Why it matters:** Tabletop, programmable access to non-Abelian gauge dynamics—normally collider-scale—opens photonic emulation of relativistic spinor physics and frequency-domain computation.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://phys.org/news/2026-07-complex-field-physics-tabletop-photonic.html

### 4. Smith-hat monotile optics: chiral diffraction from an aperiodic tile
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** University of Tokyo & collaborators (Nature Communications; covered 29 Jul 2026) nanofabricate Smith-hat monotile patterns in SiN and observe pinwheel-like chiral diffraction never seen in conventional quasicrystals. Mirrored real-space structures reverse the optical response; polarization and incidence direction further tune the pattern.
- **Why it matters:** Moves the Einstein-problem monotile from pure tiling theory into measurable light–matter physics, linking aperiodicity, broken mirror symmetry, and polarization control.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://phys.org/news/2026-07-einstein-problem-reveals-unexpected-physics.html

### 5. Calabi–Yau formality for deformation quantization
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** Ulmer (arXiv:2607.25438, 28 Jul) records the Calabi–Yau version of Kontsevich’s formality morphism. As a corollary, every unimodular holomorphic Poisson Calabi–Yau manifold admits a canonical closed deformation quantization of its Dolbeault-resolved algebra of holomorphic functions.
- **Why it matters:** Tightens the bridge between Kontsevich quantization, Calabi–Yau categories, and string-vertex motivations—still a preprint, so treat as early.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25438
