## Findings

### 1. Generating quantum entanglement from sunlight
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Li, Brar, Küblböck, Upham, Fattahi & Boyd (Optica, VoR 6 Aug 2026; DOI 10.1364/optica.601797) demonstrate polarization-entangled photon pairs from spontaneous parametric down-conversion pumped by concentrated sunlight outdoors. Quantum-state tomography yields concurrence C = 0.905 ± 0.053, purity P = 0.919 ± 0.045, Bell-state fidelity F = 0.939 ± 0.027, and a CHSH Bell violation S = 2.5408 ± 0.2171; they report a pair generation rate of order ~1600 s⁻¹ per mW of pump. An all-glass solar concentrator (Fresnel lens → hair-thin fibre) focuses highly polarized but spatially/temporally incoherent sunlight onto a millimetre-scale nonlinear crystal.
- **Why it matters:** Photonic quantum tech has long assumed laser-grade coherence for entanglement; sunlight-driven SPDC shows natural light can supply Bell-quality pairs, pointing toward lower-energy and space-deployable entanglement sources.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://doi.org/10.1364/optica.601797

### 2. Narrowband, wavelength-tuneable tabletop EUV–soft-X-ray harmonics
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Popmintchev, Imani, Roman, Wang, Yan, Song, Clairmont, Wu, Popmintchev et al. (Communications Physics, VoR 7 Aug 2026; DOI 10.1038/s42005-026-02791-5) report an efficient tabletop high-harmonic source that continuously tunes narrowband extreme-ultraviolet to soft-X-ray combs by jointly optimizing infrared spectral broadening, second-harmonic phase matching, waveguide dispersion, gas species and density. Harmonic peaks can be shifted to selected absorption edges while preserving coherence, narrow bandwidth and high brightness; in the time domain the source is expected to deliver trains of sub-300-attosecond pulses.
- **Why it matters:** Resonant imaging and spectroscopy usually need free-electron lasers; a bright, continuously tuneable lab-scale EUV/soft-X-ray comb would democratize attosecond-resonant experiments on magnetic nanostructures and related systems.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-026-02791-5

### 3. Quantum secret sharing on a triangular superconducting network
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Yan, Zang, Grebel, Wu, Chou, Andersson, Conner, Joshi et al. (npj Quantum Information, VoR 6 Aug 2026; DOI 10.1038/s41534-026-01341-9) build a three-node superconducting quantum-communication testbed with triangular topology (each edge a 1.3 m transmission line). They demonstrate pairwise state transfer and entanglement, prepare genuine tripartite GHZ entanglement, and implement quantum secret sharing of classical information, with the protocol detecting eavesdropping in situ.
- **Why it matters:** Multiparty quantum communication protocols have mostly lived in photonic links; a superconducting QSS demonstration with on-chip eavesdrop detection is a concrete step toward multiparty distributed quantum processors.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-026-01341-9

### 4. Uniform Turán densities contain a terminal interval
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** Li, Liu & Pikhurko (arXiv:2608.04790, 5 Aug) prove that the set Π_{∴,∞} of uniform Turán densities of (possibly infinite) families of 3-graphs contains a terminal interval: there exists δ > 0 such that [1−δ, 1] ⊆ Π_{∴,∞}. Consequently Π_{∴,∞} has positive Lebesgue measure and Hausdorff dimension 1.
- **Why it matters:** Uniform Turán densities organize extremal hypergraph theory; showing that densities fill a whole interval near 1 overturns sparse-set intuitions and gives a measure-theoretic foothold on which densities actually arise (preprint).
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04790

### 5. A characterization of ball quotient stacks
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** Chowdhury, Costantini & Patel (arXiv:2608.04688, 5 Aug) characterize smooth proper Deligne–Mumford stacks that arise as compactifications of ball quotient stacks [𝔹ᵈ/Γ], and show every ball quotient admits a compactification whose boundary is a disjoint union of quotient stacks [A/G] with A an abelian variety and G finite — generalizing Deng–Cadorel via Simpson/Mochizuki non-abelian Hodge correspondence in the log setting.
- **Why it matters:** Ball quotients are central complex-hyperbolic geometries; a stacky compactification characterization clarifies which orbifold boundaries can appear and links uniformization to modern non-abelian Hodge theory (preprint).
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04688
