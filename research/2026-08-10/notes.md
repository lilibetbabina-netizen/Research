## Findings

### 1. Correlated plasmons observed in a low-valence nickelate
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Shen, He, Sears, Guo, Luo, Roll, Li, Pelliciari, He, Božović and collaborators (Physical Review X, VoR 7 Aug 2026; DOI 10.1103/3ycq-jclr; preprint arXiv:2601.12160) use resonant inelastic x-ray scattering to detect plasmons in metallic trilayer Pr₄Ni₃O₈. Relative to a cuprate at comparable doping, the nickelate plasmons are more heavily damped and slower, which the authors attribute to reduced hopping and stronger screening of long-range Coulomb interactions; the modes also soften with rising temperature, unlike cuprate plasmons that stay near fixed energy while damping grows.
- **Why it matters:** Plasmon spectra encode how charges screen and move in a correlated metal. Seeing them in a nickelate superconductor parent gives a quantitative charge-dynamics benchmark that softens the often-assumed one-to-one analogy with cuprates.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://doi.org/10.1103/3ycq-jclr

### 2. Breathing ferroelectricity, flat bands and chiral phonons in Nb₃Cl₈
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Yu, Sui, Liu, Yang, Li, Ren, Chu, Yue and co-authors (Nature Communications, VoR 8 Aug 2026; DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-76538-9) report a multifaceted structural, electrical and optical study of van der Waals breathing-kagome Nb₃Cl₈. They demonstrate breathing ferroelectricity from breathing-in/out distortions of alternate kagome triangles, resolve thickness-dependent flat-band optical response (exciton-like peaks in thin flakes versus a semiconductor-like edge in thick crystals), and identify strong optical activity with multiple chiral phonons; temperature trends of phonons and flat-band features point to electronic/magnetic transitions.
- **Why it matters:** A single layered crystal that combines switchable polarization, flat electronic bands and chiral lattice modes supplies a compact platform for multifunctional quantum materials and nanoscale devices.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76538-9

### 3. Quantum causal inference from measurements alone
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Liu, Liu, Chen, Qiu, Vedral, Nie, Dahlsten & Lu (npj Quantum Information, VoR 10 Aug 2026; DOI 10.1038/s41534-026-01337-5; related preprints arXiv:2411.06051, arXiv:2411.06052) experimentally discriminate causal structures for a bipartite quantum system at two times using only coarse-grained projective measurements implemented as NMR scattering circuits on four ¹³C spins in crotonic acid. Representing the data as a space-time pseudo-density matrix, they identify structures for partial-swap and fully decohering channels without interventions that reset the system.
- **Why it matters:** Classical causal discovery usually needs interventions. Showing that light-touch quantum measurements suffice, even under spatial and temporal correlations, strengthens the case for observation-only causal inference in quantum networks and devices.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41534-026-01337-5

### 4. Composite Bogoliubov Fermi liquid in a half-filled Chern band
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Shi & Nosov (Physical Review Letters, VoR 7 Aug 2026; DOI 10.1103/wq3w-cb7f; preprint arXiv:2601.09924) argue that in inversion-asymmetric Chern bands with only C₃ rotational symmetry — the setting of recent anomalous composite Fermi liquid experiments — composite fermions can pair into a superconductor that still hosts neutral gapless Bogoliubov Fermi surfaces. The resulting composite Bogoliubov Fermi liquid is incompressible, carries quantized Hall conductance, lacks quantum oscillations versus field or doping, and retains topological ground-state degeneracy on a torus despite gapless neutral quasiparticles.
- **Why it matters:** If realized, the phase sits between a gapless composite Fermi liquid and a fully gapped Pfaffian, giving experiment a sharper target for pairing and transport diagnostics in zero-field Chern bands.
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://doi.org/10.1103/wq3w-cb7f

### 5. Modularity conjecture for higher theta series proved
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** Feng, Yun & Zhang (arXiv:2608.07173, 7 Aug) prove the Modularity Conjecture for higher theta series on moduli stacks of Hermitian shtukas. For general linear shtukas they establish a stronger “supermodularity” statement, using as a key input a Trace Conjecture for Hitchin stacks of low corank that realizes virtual fundamental classes of special cycles as categorical traces.
- **Why it matters:** Higher theta series link arithmetic geometry of shtukas to automorphic forms; proving their modularity closes a central conjectural step in that program (preprint).
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07173
