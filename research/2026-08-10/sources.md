# Sources — 2026-08-10

1. **A high-significance detection of primordial tidal torque imprints** — Sheng, Yu, Bao, Chen, Shao et al. — https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-026-02948-w — Nature Astronomy VoR 5 Aug; about 7σ spin–tidal-field alignment in gas of central massive ellipticals, with open data and code.
2. **Observation of far-from-equilibrium scaling in the transient dynamics of 2D quantum magnets** — Bensch, Borla, Balducci, Osterholz, Tang, Sheela, Chandran, Moessner, Halimeh & Groß — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07211 — 7 Aug preprint; Rydberg-array scaling collapse across four lattice geometries.
3. **Modularity of Higher Theta Series III: Proof of the Modularity Conjecture** — Feng, Yun & Zhang — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07173 — 7 Aug preprint; full modularity for higher theta series and supermodularity for general linear shtukas.
4. **A proof of the Freiman-Lev conjecture** — Wang & Tang — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04760 — 5 Aug preprint claiming the final restricted-sumset case.
5. **A Brown Theorem for Dehn functions of graphs of groups** — Llosa Isenrich & Weis — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07191 — 7 Aug preprint; quantitative combination theorem and answer to Zaremsky’s question in stated cases.
6. **Observation of Π-symmetry ultralong-range Rydberg molecules** — Eiles, Zaitsev, Dorer, Haze, Deiß, Gürleyen, Greene & Hecker Denschlag — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.07447 — 7 Aug preprint; watchlist first observation in low-n rubidium spectroscopy.
7. **Observed galaxy spin and spin reconstructed from cosmic initial condition** — Sheng et al. — https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20263913 — Reproduction data and notebook for the Nature Astronomy tidal-torque analysis.
