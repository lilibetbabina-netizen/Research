# Sources — 2026-08-12

1. **An improved direct limit on the muon electric dipole moment** — The Muon g−2 Collaboration — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11124 — 11 Aug preprint; direct 95%-confidence bound \(|d_\mu|<1.10\times10^{-19}\ e\,{\rm cm}\).
2. **Spectroscopy of Wigner crystal polarons in an atomically thin semiconductor** — Wang, Menzel, Pichler et al. — https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-026-03395-0 — Nature Physics VoR 11 Aug; optical resonances of collective Wigner-crystal excitations.
3. **Wigner polarons probe the dynamics of a Wigner crystal in a monolayer semiconductor** — Zhang, Gu, Adlong et al. — https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-026-03398-x — Nature Physics VoR 11 Aug; optical spin control and melting of a zero-field Wigner crystal.
4. **Hyper-spectral photonic integrated circuits based on recursive inverse design** — He, Tu, Wang et al. — https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-76328-3 — Nature Communications VoR 11 Aug; 11 reflective nodes match the complexity of at least 100 cascaded interferometers.
5. **Lions’ Maximal Regularity Problem for Divergence-Form Differential Operators: Failure at the 1/2-Hölder Endpoint** — Lukas Niebel — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11194 — 11 Aug preprint; scalar isotropic counterexample confirms the Auscher–Egert conjecture.
6. **Breaking the Quadratic Barrier for von Neumann Entropy Estimation** — Minbo Gao & Qisheng Wang — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11151 — 11 Aug preprint; first fixed-error \(o(d^2)\)-sample estimator.
7. **Phase coherence and disorder-induced wave propagation in micromotor arrays** — Braun, Poncet, Morin & Bartolo — https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-026-03409-x — Nature Physics VoR 11 Aug; self-organized phase coherence and disorder-launched waves.
8. **Kohayakawa’s conjecture and clique coverings of complements of paths and cycles** — Bo Ning — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11132 — 11 Aug preprint; settles Kohayakawa and de Caen–Gregory–Pullman conjectures.
9. **Assessing fidelity-limiting factors and achieving single-qubit gate fidelity beyond 99.999% in driven silicon spin qubits** — Kenta Takeda et al. — https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.11072 — 11 Aug preprint; watchlist.
