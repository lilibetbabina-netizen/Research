## Findings

### 1. Intrinsic heralding boosts non-Abelian quantum error correction
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Jing, Sala, Jiang & Verresen (Physical Review Letters; UChicago/phys.org coverage 30–31 Jul 2026) show that non-Abelian topological order can use imperfect anyon fusion products as built-in “flags” — intrinsic heralding — rather than extra flag qubits. For noise dominated by a single non-Abelian anyon type, their decoders raise the noise threshold by ~38% relative to comparable Abelian codes.
- **Why it matters:** Challenges the long-standing intuition that non-Abelian anyons trade stability for computational power; points toward more noise-tolerant topological architectures (still theory pending experiment).
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://phys.org/news/2026-07-explored-quantum-code-powerful-stable.html

### 2. Real-space braiding of Majorana-like zero modes
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Ma, He, Deng, Xiao & Liu (Nature Communications, published 31 Jul 2026) report the first direct real-space observation of braiding Majorana-like zero modes (MLZMs) in an acoustic artificial lattice. Boundary-bound MLZMs are adiabatically moved by modulating the Kekulé phase; field maps reconstruct the braiding trajectory and the characteristic phase accumulation.
- **Why it matters:** Closes the gap between proposed MLZM analogs and an experimentally watched braid — a tabletop route to studying non-Abelian exchange statistics relevant to topological quantum computation.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-75765-4

### 3. Goniopolar transverse thermoelectricity in kagome metals
- **Field:** physics
- **What changed:** Hu et al. (Nature Materials, published 31 Jul 2026) show that kagome fermiology (flat bands and van Hove singularities) drives axis-dependent carrier polarity — goniopolarity — enabling large zero-field transverse thermoelectric responses. In LuCo₆Ge₆ they measure transverse thermopower 18.4 μV K⁻¹ and transverse Peltier conductivity 105 A m⁻¹ K⁻¹ at room temperature, an order of magnitude above typical anomalous-Nernst benchmarks under zero field.
- **Why it matters:** Offers a magnetic-field-free path for transverse heat-to-electric conversion, linking quantum-materials band design to practical energy harvesting and thermal management.
- **Confidence:** high
- **Key source:** https://www.nature.com/articles/s41563-026-02678-4

### 4. White’s generic-smoothness question for area-minimizers (mod 2)
- **Field:** maths
- **What changed:** Liu (arXiv:2607.24735, v1 27 Jul / v2 28 Jul) proves that area-minimizing submanifolds in mod-2 homology are not generically smooth except for geodesics, minimal surfaces, and minimal hypersurfaces — settling White’s conjecture in the negative for higher-dimensional/codimension cases. The paper also shows the cone over the Veronese minimal embedding of ℝP² is mod-2 area-minimizing and gives a Hausdorff-dimension lower bound (d−3) on singular sets for open sets of metrics.
- **Why it matters:** Clarifies when singularities of area-minimizing cycles are unavoidable even after generic metric perturbations — a foundational geometric-measure-theory result (preprint; treat as early).
- **Confidence:** medium
- **Key source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.24735
